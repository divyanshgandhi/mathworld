## Scope
Resting Bay (`/bay`), the hub between battles. Experience mode. It is the first 2D surface of Math World's replacement visual world. A design prototype at `design/bay-accretion.html` comes before the app is ported.

## Audience and job
Kids (Grade 3+) and adults, equally. All three jobs, led by one: everything points at the next mission. The three routes (Hangar, Academy, Star Map) stay intact. The times tables stay quietly visible until touched. Wrong outcomes: anything that reads as an app or dashboard, or as generic kids-edtech.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The bay is one clear next step. The next planet leads as a lit body in the void with one Launch action; the times tables sit quietly as a strip of dots until touched. It refuses the room-with-buttons and the dashboard-of-cards. (An earlier draft drew mastery as an accretion disk; it was retired because a child could not read it.)
OWN-WORLD: void black #07070A, approach white #E6F1FF, sky #8FB4EC, disk blue #4A79BB, gold #FFC857, ember #FF6A3D, recession red #FF3E1F. Saira, thin and wide for display, medium for labels. Pill controls with a blue-to-ember hairline edge. Hairline circles and dashed orbits. Mastery marks: filled gold, filled white, blue outline, dim.
STORY: A player returns and sees at once where to go (the Moon, Launch), how they are doing (dots per table), and where else they can go (Hangar, Academy, Star map). Tapping a table opens its ten facts with plain status words and one Practise action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: the planet's name in huge thin capitals, one plain line of mission status, wave dots, the primary Launch pill, Nova's one line. Right: the planet as a lit sphere with the rocket on a dashed orbit. Bottom: the times-table strip and three place pills. A slim top band holds the wordmark, orbs, help and sound.
FORM: accretion-disk threshold (catalog challenger galaxies-nebulae-deep-time-accretion-disk-threshold, chosen by the owner over the roll; it was not on my grounded list). Seed key 896d357a.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
