# Film track, review round: the pacing pass

Branch `overhaul/r1-film`. The owner's point: "the film has too long pauses - it doesn't feel like natural dialogue between characters or between dialogues." Nothing was restaged and no word was changed. Only the film's clock moved, plus two things that had to move with it (the name card and the gates, both below).

All times are seconds on the film's own clock (the stage clock), from Play. "Sound on" means captions are paced as heard; "sound off" means they are read alone.

## What changed, by file

- `src/engine/overlay.ts`
  - New `PACE`: the limits of decision 9 as numbers (reply, same speaker, change of scene, longest silent hold, caption linger, the read-alone pace, the 5 s wait, the 1 s join).
  - `readingTime(text, heard)`: read alone is 0.8 s plus 0.42 s a word (was 1 s plus 0.67 s). Heard it is 0: a heard caption has no time of its own.
  - `scheduleCaptions(pieces, spoken, heard)`: heard, each caption has its share of the speech and the line ends with the voice. Read alone, each caption has its reading time. `hold` is never shorter than the speech.
- `src/worlds/space/pace.ts` (new): `FILM_PACE` (every lead, breath, tail and duration of the film), `ALLOWED` (the long holds and their reasons), `filmSchedule()` (the film's timeline as data).
- `src/worlds/space/intro.ts`
  - Every duration now comes from `FILM_PACE`.
  - Captions leave by themselves: 0.2 s after the voice when heard, at the end of their reading time when read alone. The reading floor that held a heard caption is gone.
  - `dialogue()` starts each line a breath after the one before it ends, and gives each beat a `cue`: the listeners turn to the next speaker 0.2 s before the first word, and the cut lands on that word.
  - Reactions happen during a line: the teacher's surprise arrives while the Captain is still saying "five cadets start today"; she is pleased while the answer is still being given.
  - Questions: the invitation shows as the question's last word lands (the end of the voice, or of the caption when it is read alone). The class waits 5 s for a hand and 5 s for an answer (the answer used to wait 8). The teacher answers 0.25 s after the cadet and 0.3 s after a classmate.
  - The knock cuts in 0.4 s after "multiplication". The door opens 1.2 s after the knock (was 1.6) and the visitors are already walking as it finishes its swing; the principal speaks 0.9 s into the walk instead of waiting for both to stop.
  - The cadet's name is called 0.5 s after the fourth name, while the last child is still getting up.
  - Launch shows as Nova's question ends, and waits 5 s (was 20).
  - Trims with no line: orbit 4.6 to 4.0, town 6.6 to 5.8, wake 3.1 to 3.0, the look to the teacher 1.8 to 1.25, the last shot 5.9 to 4.5. The yawn, the dive and the launch are untouched.
  - New option `pace: "heard" | "read"`, for timing the film while it stays muted.
  - The stills in the shot list that sat on a moved moment were re-aimed (`school`, `turn`, `door`, `names-back`, `ship`, `arrive`).
- `src/components/IntroFilm.tsx`
  - `?pace=heard` and `?pace=read` pass the option through.
  - The join into the battle is 0.5 s out and 0.5 s in (was 0.7 and 0.9), and the battle's code is fetched when the film starts, not when it ends.
  - The two gates in the mount effect and the end of a replay leave with `location.replace`. See "What I saw".
- `tests/film.test.ts`: the caption tests follow the new rules, and a new block, "film pacing", walks the schedule (below).

## Two things that moved with the clock

1. **The name card.** "Cadet <name> / Next stop: the Star Fleet hangar" used to sit alone on black for 4.3 s, inside a 9.6 s silence. A join may take a second, so the card now rises low under the cheering cadet, is read across the dip, and leaves over the hangar's first shot, 0.3 s before the Captain speaks. Same words, same shots. It is on screen for 4.0 s.
2. **The fades.** Out is 0.5 s and in is 0.5 s everywhere (they were 0.7 and 0.9). The cloud at the town clears in 0.6 s and at the last shot in 0.6 s (both were 1.0).

## APIs

```ts
// src/engine/overlay.ts
PACE                                   // { reply, same, scene, hold, linger, wait, join, read: { base, word } }
readingTime(text, heard = true)        // 0 when heard; 0.8 + 0.42 a word when read alone
scheduleCaptions(pieces, spoken, heard)

// src/worlds/space/pace.ts
FILM_PACE                              // the film's clock, as data
ALLOWED                                // the long silences and why each is allowed
filmSchedule({ heard, spoken, fits?, input }) // { lines, gaps, holds, total, join }

// src/worlds/space/intro.ts
mountIntro(stage, { ..., pace?: "heard" | "read" })
```

Nothing was renamed or removed. There is nothing for the integrator to mount: `/intro` plays the new clock as it is.

To change a pause, change the number in `FILM_PACE`. The test will say if it breaks a limit.

## The test

`tests/film.test.ts`, "film pacing". It walks `filmSchedule()` on four caption plates (wide, and two lines of 30, 36 and 42 characters), with sound on and off, played and only watched: 16 films. It fails when:

- a reply is not 0.15 to 0.45 s after the line before it, or is labelled a reply between two lines of one speaker;
- the same speaker's next thought is not 0.3 to 0.6 s after;
- any other gap between two lines, or any hold with no line over 1.2 s, is not on `ALLOWED` with a reason, or is longer than its entry;
- a wait for the player is over 5 s;
- a heard caption leaves before its voice ends or more than 0.3 s after;
- a caption read alone has less than 0.8 s plus 0.42 s a word, or is cut short by the next line;
- a fade out plus a fade in is over 1 s, or the component's fade is not the film's;
- the film is over two minutes with sound on.
