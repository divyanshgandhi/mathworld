# Glossary

These are the canonical player-facing words. Use them in copy, labels, captions and voice lines, in sentence case. A retired word is one that older screens or documents used for the same thing; replace it when you touch that text. Code identifiers may keep their old names.

| Term | What it is | Retired |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting Bay | The cadet's home hub between missions. | Cadet Bay (as the hub's name), cabin, base |
| Cadet Bay | The first training mission on Earth, at the end of the intro film. Not the hub. | Earth tutorial |
| Star map | The map of the planets, where you choose where to launch. | Solar System map, Solar map, Launch to Space (as a place) |
| Hangar | Where you spend orbs on your rocket's look. | The Hangar (on buttons), Ship upgrades shop |
| Academy | Where Nova shows any trick on a real fleet. | The Academy (on buttons), Trick films (as a place) |
| Wave | One fleet to clear. A planet's mission is a few waves. | Round, level |
| Squad | One row of ships in a fleet. `7 × 8` is 7 squads of 8 ships. | Squadron, row, group |
| Ships | What you shoot at. In Cadet Bay they are training drones. | Fighters, invaders, enemies |
| Plasma balls | What you fire. You fire exactly as many as your answer. | Shots, cannonballs, plasma cannonballs, ammo |
| Orbs | What you earn for first-try answers, Gold facts and clearing planets. Spent in the Hangar. | Orbs (capitalised mid-sentence), coins, stars, credits |
| Tricks | The ten ways to see a fact differently, each with a kid name (Double It!, Flip It!). | Trick Cards, cards, Tactical Belt |
| Power-Up | A trick used live in battle: it reshapes the fleet but never gives the answer. | Belt item, card (in battle) |
| Show Me How | Nova's worked example of a fact, after a third miss or when you ask. | Lesson, Safety net (player-facing), We Do |
| Gold | A fact you know instantly, on the first try. The top mastery level. | Gold: instant, Mastered, Ace (for a fact) |
| Got it | A fact you get right but not yet instantly. | Understood |
| Learning | A fact you are still working on. | Practising, Practicing |
| New | A fact you have not met yet. | Not met yet, Unseen, Locked (for a fact) |
| Nova | Your co-pilot, who speaks every hint and greeting. | AI, assistant, the robot |
